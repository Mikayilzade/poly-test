# 2023 naval state interactions

The December 2023 developer patch documents historical behavior for transported-unit status, hidden-unit discovery during tile interactions, and action preservation after forced movement. Source: [STEAM-POTO-BUG1-2023]. Current applicability requires verification.

The developer patch specifies that an invisible unit becomes visible if it performs Break Ice or Harvest Starfish. It also specifies that a unit carried aboard a ship receives and loses the Poison status alongside the ship. A Giant pushing a unit with Skate no longer exhausts that unit's actions. The patch removed Break Ice as an option on a frozen unit and added the carried unit's identity to the naval unit description. These are historical December 2023 observations, not current-version guarantees.
